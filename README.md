# Abstract

PawKart is an AI-powered omnichannel pet retail and inventory management platform designed to optimize product tracking, stock monitoring, and customer experience across online and offline pet retail systems.

The system integrates Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, inventory optimization, and quick-commerce technologies to solve challenges such as stockouts, inventory imbalance, delivery delays, and inefficient warehouse coordination.

PawKart enables businesses to:
- monitor inventory in real time
- predict stock shortages
- optimize warehouse operations
- support smart product substitution
- improve omnichannel synchronization
- enhance customer satisfaction

The platform also supports:
- hyperlocal delivery
- batch & expiry tracking
- warehouse coordination
- smart recommendations
- AI-driven analytics
