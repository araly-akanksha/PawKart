FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you get the most out of the PawKart platform.

How does PawKart work for different user roles? +

PawKart has custom-tailored dashboards for three specific roles. Customers can shop and track orders; Store Managers can monitor local store inventory and sales; and Admins can coordinate warehouse dispatch and site security.

Can I monitor warehouse inventory in real time? +

Yes! Our store manager and admin portals track batch codes, stock levels, and dispatch processes live. You will receive alert badges whenever items run low.

How does the delivery tracking system work? +

Once an order is created, its journey is tracked from "Ordered", "Packed", "Shipped", to "Delivered" on the customer's timeline screen, synced automatically with warehouse logistics.

Is there a mobile version of the dashboard? +

Absolutely! The storefront and all management portals are fully responsive and optimized to run beautifully on smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktops.
